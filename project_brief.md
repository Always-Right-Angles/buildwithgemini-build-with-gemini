# My agent: Wanderlust AI (Global Travel Concierge)
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps travelers design personalized vacation itineraries with a catalog of curated global destinations, activities, and scenic photo previews.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers traveler profile (budget tier, travel style, party size, dietary/accessibility preferences, and past destinations visited).
- Tools: Searches destinations and attractions, checks local weather/seasonal suitability, and structures day-by-day itineraries.
- Catalog/UI: Curated collection of destinations, sights, and itinerary day cards with duration, costs, and ratings (rendered as A2UI rich cards and tables).
- Image gen: Generates scenic teaser postcards and visual previews of planned destinations and highlights.
- Sandbox: Computes total trip budget breakdowns, currency conversions, and per-person cost allocations.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for budget & currency math, Google Maps integration for routing, Cloud Trace for latency tracking.
